What the software does to your mailbox

Gmail Agent moves mail to Trash, archives it, adds stars and labels, and creates calendar events on your behalf, based on rules you set and automated classification. It never permanently deletes mail, and it never sends a message without showing you the exact recipient, subject, and body and asking for confirmation. Automated classification is not perfect; review a dry run before applying one, and use gmail undo to reverse a run.
