Gmail Agent

Connect Gmail to use Gmail Agent in your terminal.

This page was not opened by Gmail Agent, so it cannot connect anything on its own.

Install it and start setup from your terminal:

npm install --global gmail-agent-cli
gmail setup

That command opens this page again with a live session.

What it can do with your Google account

Gmail Agent can read and organize your mail, move messages to Trash, archive them, add stars and labels, and prepare replies for your review. It can also see, create, change, and delete events on Google calendars you own, and it uses that only for the calendar behavior described in its documentation.

It never permanently deletes mail, and it never sends a message without showing you the exact recipient, subject, and body and asking first.

Included AI

During a preview or cleanup, unresolved messages may be classified automatically — not only messages you pick one at a time. For each such message, Gmail Agent may send the sender name and address, the subject, the date, your timezone, a bulk-mail signal, your Gmail label names, and bounded plain text from the body or Gmail's preview snippet through [Publisher] to [Model service and subprocessors].

Drafting sends the message you are replying to, or the recipient, subject, and purpose you typed. This launch version does not send recent Sent mail to learn your writing style. Attachments, inline images, and your Google credentials are never sent.

This data is used only for these visible features — never for generalized training, ranking, categorization, or any other secondary purpose. Fair-use limits apply; when the allowance runs out, Gmail Agent keeps working with rules only.

Without hosted AI, no message text leaves your computer. Gmail's own spam, your own spam and important rules, and archiving still work; anything needing judgment is left for you. You can turn AI on later, with the included service or your own API key.
