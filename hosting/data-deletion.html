Data deletion

How to remove everything, on your computer and on ours.

On your computer

Disconnect the account. This revokes the Google grant where possible, erases the stored refresh token and the included-AI session, and asks whether to keep local history:

gmail setup   # then choose "Disconnect Gmail from this computer"

To remove the local cache of message metadata as well:

gmail uncache

Everything the CLI stores lives in one per-user directory (~/Library/Application Support/gmail-agent-cli on macOS, %APPDATA%\\gmail-agent-cli on Windows, $XDG_CONFIG_HOME/gmail-agent-cli elsewhere). Deleting that directory removes the configuration, the local database, and the diagnostic logs. Secrets live in your OS credential store, not in that directory, which is why disconnecting is the step that erases them.

On our side

We hold two things about you: a consent receipt and usage counters, both keyed to a pseudonymous identifier and neither containing your email address or any message content.

Disconnecting revokes the session and marks the entitlement revoked. To have the records themselves deleted, email [Privacy contact address] from the Google account you connected, or quote the pseudonymous identifier the CLI shows in gmail doctor. We will confirm and delete within [Deletion SLA].

Google’s own copy

Revoking access at myaccount.google.com/permissions ends the app’s authorization independently of anything above. Mail in Gmail’s Trash is deleted by Gmail on its own schedule; this app never deletes mail permanently.
